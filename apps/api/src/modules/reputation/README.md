# Reputation module: the reputation builder (REP-001)

Every participant builds and confirms their own reputation from evidence ([ADR 010](../../../../../docs/adr/010-gathering-point-hubs-agents-and-mesh.md) decision 18). Rules: [reputation builder and passport](../../../../../docs/engine/modules/reputation-and-passport.md). Contracts: [`packages/contracts/src/reputation-builder.ts`](../../../../../packages/contracts/src/reputation-builder.ts). Storage: [`packages/data/migrations/reputation`](../../../../../packages/data/migrations/reputation). Snapshots stay EXE-001's ([trust module](../trust/README.md)); nothing here changes a snapshot, and there is no public numeric score (O07).

All mutations: authenticated session, `X-Trustwork-Request` with same-origin metadata, `Idempotency-Key`; every route completes its idempotency record inside its own request transaction (BE-005). Errors use the contract envelope; specific reasons are in `details.reason`. A refusal that limits the person also carries `details.limitation`: reason code, explanation key (`reputation.limitation.<code>`), evidence and an appeal link.

| Route | Who | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /v1/reputation-builder/:kind/:id/own` | The subject (the person, or a member of the company with `company.deals.manage`) | Credentials with derived status and checks, portfolio with "self-reported" or "client confirmed", requests sent, contributions and timeliness derived from canonical records (each with its limitation and appeal link), recorded limitations, own declared relationships |
| `GET /v1/reputation-builder/:kind/:id/inbox` | The addressed party | Confirmation requests addressed to it |
| `GET /v1/reputation-builder/:kind/:id` | Anyone | Public credentials (kind, type, issuer, status, check date and hub) and consented portfolio work; never numbers, documents, counterparties, statements or amounts; nothing for a person with a private profile |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/credentials` | The holder | Licence, insurance, bond, certificate, training, membership or registration; corrections append (`supersedesId`) |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/credentials/:id/checks` | The holder | The hub's register lookup through `CredentialRegisterPort` (only `FakeCredentialRegister` exists; read-only, bounded by a timeout; `unavailable` stays unknown) |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/portfolio` | The holder | Work on a context it took part in (accepted agreement, completed task, closed project) or an external description (self-reported forever); no address field exists |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/relations` | A person | Declares a relative, household or close personal relationship; blocks confirmations between the two; private to the declarer |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/confirmations` | The requester | Only to the other side of a recorded relationship; never to oneself, an employee (current or former) or a relative; once per counterparty, context and statement; at most 20 a day; attests no incentive and no conflict; 30-day deadline |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/confirmations/:id/reminder` | The requester | The single reminder, three days after the request, before an answer or the deadline |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/confirmations/:id/withdraw` | The requester | Withdraws an unanswered request (it is never sent again) |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/confirmations/:id/response` | A person acting for the addressed party; no actor field | `confirmed`, `partially` or `declined` (with a reason), consent to show; final, appealable |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/appeals` | The subject | A counterparty's answer (DATA-002 queue, original decider the responder), a timeliness or contribution record (appeals its source: EXE-001's `trust_file_appeal` on the performance observation, or the answer) |
| `POST /v1/reputation-builder/limitations/appeals` | The refused person | Re-evaluates the refusal, records the `automated_limitation` with its evidence and files the appeal; decided by a trust reviewer who is not the appellant |

Contributions (`consultation_given` from a confirmed `advice_delivered` answer, and from completed consultation agreements once CONS-001 opens them) and timeliness (from EXE-001 timing and payment observations, with client-caused and external changes excluded) are written only by `trustwork.reputation_derive`, run when the subject reads its own view. The subject of every record is the party of the context, never an employee; no session, login, reading, follow, assistant, audit or membership data is an input. Registry entries: `REPUTATION_BUILDER_MANIFEST` (module `reputation_builder`); in this slice every commit is confirmed by the person each time, and delegation under mandates arrives with AGT-005.
