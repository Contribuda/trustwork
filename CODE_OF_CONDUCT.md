# Code of conduct

We build infrastructure that people will trust with their professional reputation. The same standard applies to how we treat each other.

## Expected

- Be direct and respectful. Criticise work, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- Give credit, cite sources, say what you do not know.
- Keep private data private: no personal data of real people in issues, fixtures, logs or screenshots.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information or unverified claims about real people or companies.
- Fabricating evidence: fake users, fake verifications, fake test results.

## Scope and enforcement

Applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, chats and events connected to the project. Report concerns to the maintainers listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`; reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, temporarily or permanently exclude participants who break these rules. Decisions can be appealed to the project owner.
