## Location and sanctions compliance

We apply location restrictions and compliance checks to determine whether we may provide this service. A location estimate can be incorrect. Contact {operator compliance contact} if you believe a restriction is mistaken.

The location gate uses a network address transiently with {actual configured location adapter and dataset}. It does not retain that address in its compliance records, logs or notification payloads. Records may contain the restricted place, the source of the signal, dataset reference, time, hold and the evidence and reason for a later review. The operator must separately document the actual network infrastructure's logging and retention; this template does not make promises about an unreviewed proxy or hosting provider.

Compliance checks may process account or company names and compare them with the operator's legally required sources. A possible match is not a finding about the person. Human review, decisions, reasons and evidence are recorded. Commercial commitments and payment instructions may be paused while the required checks or reviews remain unresolved. The engine does not hold participants' money.

The participant sees only the information the applicable law permits. Notices do not identify a source entry, a matched name, another person or a reviewer. In the absence of an authoritative notice-disclosure policy, the service uses a generic contact notice. Other participants receive no compliance explanation about you. These records are not public reputation signals, ranking inputs, search results or federation data.

You may request review through the account compliance page when a decision is eligible for appeal, after confirming your identity again. The appeal records your explanation and relevant evidence; do not submit passwords or unrelated personal information. The reviewer must differ from the decider of the appealed decision. A reversal needs a second person. Filing an appeal is not confirmation that a reviewer has been assigned, that a restriction was lifted or that a deadline has been promised. Location restrictions continue to apply; the account page is not an exception to the restricted-place gate. If access is unavailable, use {operator compliance contact}.

The operator must insert the actual purposes, lawful bases, recipients, permitted disclosures, retention and erasure rules, rights and complaint channels for {applicable jurisdictions}. Retained legal records may remain after account erasure where required, with pseudonymization where permitted. The operator must verify implementation and its approved retention policy before publishing any period or erasure promise.
