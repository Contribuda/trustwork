Sources

The book's own sources are Trustwork's record: its decision records, handbook chapters, status page and launch playbooks. Those need no link here and are named without repository paths. Below are the outside facts the book relies on, grouped by theme, each with its publisher, title, year and link, read on 4 November 2026 unless another date is given, with its evidence level where it matters; and, at the end, what the sources leave open. A plain entry is a primary or official source; "secondary" marks press, encyclopaedias, law-firm notes and aggregators; "self claim" marks what an organisation says about itself. The Corruption Perceptions Index 2025 and the FATF lists were read only from secondary summaries and appear in the book as reported.
