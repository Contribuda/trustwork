An invitation

This page is the invitation.

We are looking for people and organisations who do one thing well and want it to leave a record. Associations, chambers, publishers, universities, training bodies, cooperatives and founders who want to run a hub for their country or their city, under their own law, with a team of agents from the first day and people as they grow. Municipalities, counties, audit offices and civic bodies that already publish a budget and a project list and would like to see one cycle of it followed from the budget line to the building, in public, whether it flatters or not. Licensed lenders, housing-finance institutions, insurers, escrow providers, inspectors and valuers who would read a standard, evidenced build file and release, insure or confirm a stage against it. Professional bodies and training centres whose certificates deserve to be checkable by number and to travel with the worker. Manufacturers and suppliers who would rather be found on the step of a plan where they help than pay for a place at the top of a list. Newsrooms that name their sources. Funders who want to pay for work done in the open. Developers, designers, translators, lawyers and practitioners, with or without an AI agent they are accountable for, who want to build an engine others will run. And people who know a country well enough to maintain its facts with sources.

What we offer is the thing this book describes, with no licence fee and no implementation fee: a gathering point your industry will come back to; a record of who built what, how well, for whom and with whose money, that nobody can buy; one representative agent per person, under mandates you can read; a country pack that makes the engine correct where you are; a mesh that makes your market larger from the first day; honesty about what is Live and what is Proposed; and lines we will never cross, in every country, for anyone.

What we ask is what chapter 22 sets out: a real first case, a named person, clear terms, and acceptance that evidence is published with a right of reply and never a right of deletion. The civic ring opens only where residents can use it safely; the build file is read only with the project owner's consent; the vote belongs to a neutral keeper. Nothing in this book is a promise that any government, bank or body has agreed to anything. The first partner in every country will be the one whose real case comes first.

The way in is a conversation. Write to Contribuda, say who you are, which place you work in and what you do there, and name the first case you would want to run with us: one municipality, one build, one province's project list, one course, one country pack. If the case is real, we will plan it together, in the open, as the first record between us.

Bring one real place. We will lay the first brick together.
