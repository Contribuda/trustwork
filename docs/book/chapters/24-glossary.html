Glossary

The words of Trustwork, as this book uses them. A reader who knows them can read any chapter and tell at once what is built, what is proposed in the repository and what is still a direction of this book.

Acceptance
A person's recorded decision that a delivery, a stage or a work meets its criteria, taken on the evidence. The mortar between the bricks: nothing counts towards reputation, and no stage is released, without it.
Accessory dwelling unit (ADU)
A second, smaller home on the lot of an existing house, such as a backyard cottage or a converted garage; in San Diego, the kind of private build the reference hub's scenes describe (chapters 10, 17 and 20).
ADR
An architecture decision record: a dated record of a product decision, with its context, the decision in numbered points and its consequences. Proposed until accepted.
Baukultur
The culture of building well, as defined by the Davos Declaration of 2018 and measured by the Davos Baukultur Quality System's eight criteria: governance, functionality, environment, economy, diversity, context, sense of place and beauty. A tool a hub may adopt, never a standard imposed.
Build file
The standard, evidenced record of one private build, assembled from the plan, permits, named professionals, frozen quotes, inspection gates, field evidence, acceptance, change and defect records and the contractor's reputation, released only with the owner's consent so that a licensed lender or insurer can read one format. A direction not yet designed; chapter 10.
Case grant
The scoped, recorded permission under which a hub staff member may read a private record for one case. Without it, private stays private, including from staff; there is no "log in as user".
Civic engine
The civic ring of the same engine: participatory and initiative budgeting, open budgets, open contracting, public-works records, institutions' delivery records and the learning around them, grown out of construction. A direction not yet designed; a decision record is expected.
Civic hub
A hub, or a part of one, run for a city's public works and participation alongside or inside an industry hub, where the civic-space gate is open. A municipality running one itself is a further step, to be decided in the civic decision record.
Civic-space gate
The rule that a hub may enable the civic ring only in a territory whose current civic-space rating and open-government standing meet a threshold to be set by a recorded decision, with date and source in the country pack and re-checked yearly. A gate, not a score.
CIVICUS Monitor
The civil-society research network's rating of civic space in every country, from open through narrowed, obstructed and repressed to closed, with the date of each change. One of the two inputs of the civic-space gate, recorded in the country pack with its source.
Claimed page
A sourced page that a verified login with one tie to the company has taken responsibility for. Between sourced and verified.
Co-financing
Residents or local businesses paying a share of a project's cost, in money or in kind. The signature of initiative budgeting. In the engine: optional, valued at a published rate, capped, disclosed, and never a condition for the poorest neighbourhoods to be served.
Company representative
The one standing agent a company may have, acting under mandates with a named accountable person. Not a swarm; not a panel.
Connected, planned
The two partner words, and the rule beside them. Connected: a real integration that works today. Planned: a work package names it, and it is not integrated yet. Every other organisation in this book is named for its public work; naming implies no contact, interest or agreement, and nobody is called a partner without an agreement both sides have made public. Nothing is connected today.
Consultation
A scoped question from a plan item answered by an expert for a fee, with a record. In the civic ring, professional feasibility and costing before a vote is a consultation.
Contribuda
The company that creates and maintains Trustwork and gives it away under its licence, with no licence or implementation fee and no share of any hub's domestic income; it takes part only in the international layer (chapter 7). Nobody needs it to run a hub; at a hub owner's wish it may be the tool for running one, on its own terms (chapter 23). The decided operator of the reference hub. Formerly Contribuddha; public contact addresses keep the domain contribuddha.com until the move to the new name is complete.
CoST
The Infrastructure Transparency Initiative: disclosure of forty data points across a public project's life, independent assurance and an infrastructure transparency index. Its disclosures become sourced pages; its assurance report is a format a hub's magazine can adopt.
Counterparty confirmation
The other side's recorded statement that a fact happened as claimed: a delivery received, a payment made, a work accepted. Asked once plus one reminder, never with an incentive.
Country pack
A country's facts as data with sources: identifiers, regions, currencies, languages, registers, licensing, codes, procurement, tax, signatures, payments, holidays, and the civic facts the loop needs. Maintained by a country-pack maintainer, who is the source of truth for them.
Decennial insurance
Insurance against structural defects for ten years after completion, priced where it exists from inspected construction. In this book, the long-run shape of an insurer pricing from evidence; a question for licensed insurers and counsel in every country.
Delivery record
See institution's delivery record.
Demonstration hub, exemplary hub
See reference hub.
Digital public good
Open-source software, data, standards or content that meets the Digital Public Goods Standard's nine indicators and is listed in its registry. The engine meets most; the gaps are documentation and policy; it is not listed and will not call itself one until it is.
Direction of this book
A design that exists in this book and not yet in the repository's handbook, decision records or work packages: the civic engine, the build file, the house record, the owner's adviser, the observatory, the synchronisation with Contribuda. Not a status word; the repository's status page is authoritative for what is built, and a direction becomes Proposed only when the repository designs it.
Disbursement instruction
The record the engine composes when a stage gate closes on its three records, telling the licensed lender or escrow provider that the stage is accepted. The provider releases the money in its own systems; the engine never holds it. A direction not yet designed; chapter 10.
Draw inspection
An independent inspector's visit and act confirming that a construction stage is complete as planned, against which a lender releases the next draw. In the engine, the inspector's act is a record with the inspector's name and reputation attached.
Engine
Trustwork itself: free software under AGPL-3.0-only that anyone may run as a node, hosting one or more hubs, joined to others in one mesh.
FATF list
The Financial Action Task Force's list of jurisdictions under increased monitoring, often called the grey list. A country's standing on it is a sourced fact in its country pack, which licensed payment providers weigh; it bars nothing in the engine.
Field evidence
Dated photographs with grid references, measurements, delivery notes and acts gathered on site, attached to a stage, a work or a public project. In the civic ring, a resident's observation is field evidence too, pseudonymous by default: only the hub's trust reviewer knows who made it, and where protection is weak it can be made through an institution.
Frozen quote
A quote that cannot be changed once sent; compared on equal terms; the winning one becomes an agreement both sides can prove identical.
House record
The record of one house for as long as it stands: the build file as its first chapter, then the maintenance plan, every repair and renovation with its quote and acceptance, the sale and the inheritance. Owned by the family, passed with the home, every entry showing who confirmed it, exportable in an open format, read by a lender or insurer only with consent. Never a score. A direction not yet designed; chapter 2.
Hub
The platform for one territory, a country, a region or a city, run by a hub operator on a node. Territories are descriptive and never exclusive; nobody owns a city; a restricted place is never a territory.
Hub operating company
The small company around a hub: 23 functions, each with an accountable person, run from the first day by the operator's agent team and staffed with people as the hub grows. An accepted decision; function accountability and the staff lifecycle are Live in the API, the function catalogue and team presets an open work package.
Hub operator
The legal entity responsible for a hub: an association, a chamber, a publisher, a university, a training body, a cooperative, an entrepreneur or a community. Screened against sanctions lists; accountable to its own law and counsel.
Hub owner
The person, company or public body on a country's side that owns a hub and its income. Often the hub operator itself; see hub operator.
Initiative budgeting
The variant of participatory budgeting in which residents and local business co-finance the project, framing it as a joint venture of community and state. Named after a World Bank programme; practised at scale in Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan.
Inspector
A licensed or recognised person who confirms a stage or a work on site and signs an act. A participant with a reputation from their own acts, never an employee of the hub.
Inspector's act
The independent inspector's signed record of what they saw at a stage, separate from the builder's statement and the owner's acceptance; the third record that closes a gate. A direction not yet designed; chapter 10.
Institution's delivery record
A public institution's page of planned against executed: budget lines, projects promised, contracted, built, accepted and stalled, documents published and when, confirmations by residents and inspectors as separate records, with the institution's reply. Built only from public, sourced data; never a score; never a ranking of officials. A direction not yet designed.
Integrator
The maintainer role that reviews and merges, and keeps decisions, work packages and acceptance evidence coherent.
International layer
Deals and transactions between parties on hubs in different countries, and reputation across the mesh. The only place where Contribuda takes part, in a form to be decided (chapter 7); disclosed to both parties, never affecting ranking and never touching a domestic deal.
Launch checklist
The twelve gates the engine refuses to skip before a hub goes public, from a screened operator and counsel-approved texts to a restore drill and an accessibility check. Soft launch needs the first six and two more.
Loop of public trust
The book's central civic figure: a resident's idea, discussion and co-design, feasibility and cost by professionals, a vote, a line in an open budget, an open tender, construction with field evidence, confirmation by residents and inspectors, a record and reputation from evidence, lessons shared, and the next idea. As a module, a direction not yet designed; chapter 4.
Magazine
A hub's curated weekly of sourced news and guides, released by the editor-in-chief, with corrections published. In the civic ring, the place for "what was promised, what was built".
Mandate
The readable, changeable, revocable grant under which a person's representative agent may act, confirmed only by the person on a first-party form. Chat text never authorises a commitment.
Model Context Protocol (MCP)
The open protocol through which an AI chat connects to outside tools. In Trustwork, the way a person reaches their representative agent from the chat they already use, under the hub's authorisation server and the person's mandates. The authorisation server is Live; the transport is an open work package.
Mesh
The worldwide federation of nodes. Public projections federate by default; deal data travels only between the parties' nodes; private data never leaves its node. One mesh, many owners.
Node
One installation of the engine: a database, a web origin, a signing key and an operator. A node hosts one or more hubs.
Observatory (of defects)
A hub's consented, aggregated statistics on defects, cost and duration read over the life of houses, not only their build, by region, building type, stage and element, with small cells suppressed and no addresses, modelled on the national defect observatories of France, Japan and Denmark. Lets insurers price, lenders lend and a hub see whether its courses work. The family's record is never the statistic. A direction not yet designed.
Open budget
A government's budget documents published in full and on time, measured by the Open Budget Survey in transparency, participation and oversight. In the loop, the source of the budget line a voted project becomes.
Open contracting
Publishing every stage of public procurement, from planning to implementation, in open data; the Open Contracting Data Standard is its format and OC4IDS its extension for infrastructure projects. Open contracting data becomes sourced tender, contract and public-works pages.
Operator agents
The operator's agent team: hub automation for editorial, data, support, trust, compliance and the other functions, under operator grants, with a named accountable person per role, budgets and kill switches. Not participants; no accounts, no mandates, no public presence.
Owner
The project's owner, Dmitry Parshkov, distinct from a hub owner and from the owner of a building project, who holds the copyright and the brand and has the final say on product constraints; decisions are recorded and dated in the ADRs.
Owner's adviser
A person on a family's side who knows building, a retired master, a registered technical entity, a municipal engineer, acting under a mandate from the family to inspect stages, record defects and confirm acceptances. A participant with a reputation from their own confirmations, paid by the family or a programme, never by the builder. A direction not yet designed.
Participatory budgeting
A process in which residents propose, discuss and vote on how a share of a public budget is spent, usually on built things. Begun in Porto Alegre in 1989; mandatory by law in Peru, the Dominican Republic and South Korea; practised in thousands of cities. Its weakest link everywhere is delivery.
Peering
The double-opt-in connection between two nodes of the mesh, recorded on both sides, with a second approver where deals or identity are routed, graded by what the two nodes agree to exchange, suspended when a peer's attestation lapses and ended without rewriting history.
Plan to people
The journey from "I want to build a house" to phases, trades, permits, budget ranges and risks, and from plan items to consultations with experts and tasks for trades; finished projects become shared knowledge with the owner's consent.
Project committee
Residents elected in a public forum to oversee one public project, as Makueni's project management committees, Peru's vigilance committees, the Dominican works committees and Mexico City's execution and oversight committees. In the engine their confirmation is a record separate from the contractor's and the inspector's.
Public projection
The public, consent-filtered view of a record, a profile, a company page, a published project or a sourced page, with no private fields and no counts; the only thing that federates by default.
Public-works record
A sourced page for one public project with its budget line, contracts, milestones, field evidence, resident observations and inspector or client confirmations, each with its status and source, shaped on OC4IDS. A direction not yet designed.
Recognition of prior learning
Assessment of skills a worker already has by a recognised body, ending in a certificate. For the informal builder, the first rung of formality; a hub is a place where it can be recorded once, portably.
Reference hub
The United States hub with San Diego as home territory. Contribuda is its decided operator; it is demo-only on synthetic data and not deployed for real use. By the project owner's recorded decision it is the engine's demonstration and exemplary hub, where every new method, technology and function lands first, with ownership to be transferred by open competition to a local operator on terms to be decided.
Refinance company
A licensed institution that refinances many lenders' mortgages against its own eligibility criteria (Kenya's KMRC, Nigeria's NMRC, Uzbekistan's refinancing company). The most influential reader of a build file: a file in its criteria is read by every lender it refinances.
Representative agent
The one agent each person has, acting under mandates the person can read, change and revoke. It works for you, not on you. It will connect your own AI chat over MCP; the authorisation server is Live and the transport is an open work package.
Reputation
What the engine computes from evidence: accepted deliveries, confirmed acts, defects remedied, payments on time, reported in separate dimensions with sample sizes and a published method, never as one public score, never bought. Absence of evidence is unknown, not failure.
Reputation passport
A person's portable, signed, revocable record of certificates and confirmed work, issued by the home hub as verifiable credentials, with the holder choosing what to include. Proposed.
Resident
A person who lives in a place, whether or not a citizen. The book's default word; "citizen" appears only where a law or a programme uses it. A resident is never the object of a record.
Restricted place
A territory the engine refuses at every level under sanctions law: Russia, Crimea, Sevastopol, the Donetsk and Luhansk regions, Cuba, Iran and North Korea. Never a territory, never a host, never a market.
Sealed request for quotes
A request whose quotes stay sealed until the deadline: no late undercutting; the buyer sees who answered but no price until then. The private-sector analogue of a sealed public bid.
Second-person activation
The rule that sensitive changes, a live provider, prices, a dataset with personal data, the release of a kill switch, need a second staff member with a distinct role to take effect.
Social audit
A public verification of a programme's records by trained residents, ending in a public hearing with the responsible officials present. Credible when an independent, funded body convenes it and consequences follow.
Sourced page
A page about a company, a tender, a contract, a public work or a building built from a public source, labelled "from an open source" with field-level provenance, licence and date. Nothing on it is a Trustwork judgement.
Staged disbursement
Money released by a licensed lender, escrow provider or funder stage by stage against accepted evidence and an inspector's act. The engine exposes a release signal and never holds the money.
Statuses
The engine's five words for what exists: Live (implemented end to end with tests, not yet deployed to production anywhere), Demo (an interface on synthetic data), Contract (data shapes frozen as tested schemas), Designed (an accepted design, no code), Proposed (designed in the handbook for the first time, with work packages, open for review). See also direction of this book.
Task
A piece of work from a plan item given to a trade or a worker, accepted against criteria, and the smallest unit from which an informal worker's portfolio is built.
Territory
A hub's declared country, region or city, identified by standard codes and a place key. Descriptive, never exclusive.
Tombstone
A signed record that something was deleted or unpublished, which every peer applies so that a withdrawn projection disappears across the mesh, and which is replayed after a restore so that nothing deleted comes back.
Valuer
A licensed professional who states what an asset, finished or unfinished, is worth. For a lender, the person the build file was written to help; in the engine, a participant with a reputation from their own acts.
Verified page
A claimed page whose register record is active and matching, with a signatory tie and sanctions screening. An uploaded extract or a registration number alone proves nothing.
Vote module
The engine's participatory ballot: approval, knapsack or equal-shares counting, district envelopes, a verified-once pseudonymous ballot that cannot be linked to a person by any role, identity proven outside the hub, and a neutral keeper who runs it, a municipality, an electoral institute or an independent civic body, never an industry hub operator. A direction not yet designed.
Warranty insurance
Cover for defects in a finished build for a stated period, priced by an insurer from the build file's evidence of who built what and how it was inspected. A possible outcome of chapter 10, never a Trustwork product.
Work package
A public unit of engineering work with a goal, acceptance criteria and owned paths, claimed on its issue and accepted by review. The civic engine has none until its decision record lands.
