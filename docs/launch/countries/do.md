# Dominican Republic: deep dive and launch plan

Rank 6 of 10 (score 78, estimate, medium uncertainty; [scorecard](../../research/top-10-launch-countries-2026-11.md#scorecard)). Status: **Proposed** playbook. Nothing here is implemented or legal advice; every legal point is a lead for counsel. Sources were accessed on 2026-10-31 unless stated; *(secondary)* marks press, law-firm notes and aggregators. Read the [generic playbook](../README.md) first.

## Summary

The Dominican Republic is **small (11.5 million people) but unusually ready**: 91 percent of people are online, construction is a large share of the economy, Spanish is a live interface language, the tax authority publishes the taxpayer list, and the procurement directorate publishes OCDS data daily under a new procurement law in force since January 2026. Industry bodies already worry about the qualification of construction labour and run training and platforms for workers, which makes them natural partners for worker credentials and plan-step guides. No national platform for owned reputation or plans was found. Weak points: construction contracted in 2025 after a strong start, the data protection law is older and lighter, and the central bank's new instant-payment system is planned for 2027.

**Segments.** (1) *Residential* by developers (apartments, low-cost housing programmes); (2) *tourism construction* (hotels and villas on the east coast); (3) *self-built and improved homes*, often financed from abroad; (4) *public works* through the procurement system; (5) *commercial and free-zone industrial*.

## 2. Pain points

| Who | Pain | What Trustwork offers |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowners and families abroad | Unreliable builders; no proof of progress; disputes over money sent for materials | Plans with budget ranges; remote owner oversight with a delegated representative (PLAN-005); quotes frozen once sent |
| Master builders and workers | Skills not recognised; a law reserves the title of master builder to diploma holders, but most practical skill is uncertified | Profiles, client-confirmed portfolios, training-institute certificates as credentials (REP-003) |
| Developers and contractors | Labour shortages; public tenders under a new law | Sourced pages from public data; opportunities from OCDS; worker discovery that never filters by nationality |
| Public buyers | New procurement law and its gradual implementation | Labelled opportunities and track records |

## 4. Culture and trust

- **Finding builders:** family and neighbours, the "maestro" known in the barrio, hardware stores, WhatsApp and Facebook. Families in the United States, Spain and elsewhere fund homes and want proof of progress.
- **Digital habits:** 10.5 million internet users (91.0 percent), 10.6 million connections, Facebook 6.1 million, YouTube 7.5 million, TikTok almost all adults ([DataReportal Digital 2026 Dominican Republic](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-dominican-republic)).
- **Language and literacy:** Spanish; adult literacy 94.0 percent (World Bank WDI, 2024); English common in tourism.
- **Inclusion:** the construction workforce includes many migrant workers; the hub never asks for or infers nationality for matching, ranking or recommendations, and follows counsel on labour-law questions.
- **Calendar:** national holidays to be sourced in CTRY-DO; the hurricane season (roughly June to November) affects work and insurance.

## 8. Infrastructure realities

Very high internet use; good urban networks; hurricanes and power cuts call for offline drafts. WhatsApp for consented notifications. Addresses vary in quality outside cities (ADDR-001).

## 9. Partners to approach (kinds only)

Housing developers' association; the engineers', architects' and surveyors' college; the cement makers' association; the national technical training institute; Habitat for Humanity and similar housing NGOs; universities; diaspora associations.

### Agent team tasks specific to the Dominican Republic

- **Discovery:** construction companies from the RNC list (activity codes in CTRY-DO) linked to DGCP awards by RNC; manufacturers and suppliers from public sites.
- **Editorial:** new procurement law explainers for small contractors, e-CF deadlines, hurricane-resistant building guides.
- **Families abroad (PLAN-005):** weekly evidence digests in the owner's language and time zone.

### Outreach policy settings

`strict`; counsel to advise on Law 172-13 and any anti-spam rules before page notices. No SMS, WhatsApp or calls without recorded prior consent.

### Costs (estimates)

The [generic cost model](../README.md#costs), low end (one language, a small market, structured data).

### KPIs

Generic KPIs plus: companies linked to DGCP awards; remote-owner projects; master builders with training-institute credentials.

### Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Labour-nationality debates | Never collect or use nationality for matching or ranking; labour questions to counsel |
| Small market economics | Peer with other Spanish-language hubs; low-cost hosting; partners share editorial work |
| Hurricanes | Offline drafts; flexible deadlines |

### Approvals before going live

Operator and screening; counsel on terms, privacy notice (Law 172-13), master-builder labelling, RNC and DGCP reuse, outreach mode, agent disclosure; provider activations; the hub owner's switch (and the repository owner's for a Contribuda-operated hub).

### Feature gaps for the Dominican Republic

CTRY-DO, PLAN-005, REP-003, PROC-003 (DGCP OCDS), REG-002 (RNC list and suppliers register), PAY-003.
