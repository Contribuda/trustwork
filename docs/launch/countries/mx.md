# Mexico: deep dive and launch plan

Rank 9 of 10 (score 72, estimate, medium uncertainty; [scorecard](../../research/top-10-launch-countries-2026-11.md#scorecard)). Status: **Proposed** playbook. Nothing here is implemented or legal advice; every legal point is a lead for counsel. Sources were accessed on 2026-10-31 unless stated; *(secondary)* marks press, law-firm notes and aggregators. Read the [generic playbook](../README.md) first.

## Summary

Mexico is a **large Spanish-speaking market where self-build dominates housing**: INEGI's housing satellite account puts self-construction at 55.6 percent of the housing market by value. 110 million people are online, Spanish is a live interface language and a country pack is already planned (CTRY-MX, amended here with the playbook's leads). The cycle is weak (construction GDP down about 1 percent in 2025, civil works down sharply, public works at their lowest since records began) and lead marketplaces such as Habitissimo and Cronoshare are present, so the competition rating is 3. The opportunity is the self-builder and the families abroad who pay for homes, with plans, guides and evidence-based reputation for maestros. A new federal data protection law took effect in March 2025 and a new authority replaced the former transparency institute.

**Segments.** (1) *Self-build and progressive housing*, the largest by value; (2) *social housing* under federal programmes; (3) *industrial and logistics* construction in the north (nearshoring); (4) *public works*, currently depressed; (5) *renovation*.

## 4. Culture and trust

- **Finding builders:** family and compadres, the maestro who built a neighbour's house, hardware stores, Facebook groups. Many homes are financed by relatives in the United States.
- **Digital habits:** 110 million internet users (83.5 percent), 145 million connections, Facebook ad reach 93.5 million, Messenger 54.6 million, median mobile download 45.21 Mbps ([DataReportal Digital 2026 Mexico](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-mexico)). WhatsApp is the everyday business channel.
- **Language and literacy:** Spanish; many Indigenous languages; adult literacy 95.6 percent (World Bank WDI, 2024).
- **Security:** extortion affects construction in some states; never expose active site locations or values (ADDR-001).
- **Calendar:** national holidays, Holy Week, 16 September, Day of the Dead, December holidays (to be sourced in CTRY-MX); the rainy season (roughly June to October) and hurricanes on the coasts.

### Agent team tasks specific to Mexico

- **Discovery:** state and municipal OCDS awards for track records; INEGI's business directory only where the licence allows and only for businesses, never individuals.
- **Editorial:** self-build guides with partner material; public works and housing programme news; nearshoring construction news in the north.
- **Families abroad:** PLAN-005 digests in Spanish and English.

### Outreach policy settings

`strict`; counsel to advise on the 2025 law and anti-spam rules before any notice. No SMS, WhatsApp or calls without recorded prior consent.

The [generic cost model](../README.md#costs); Spanish only; support across time zones for families abroad.

Generic KPIs plus: progressive plans created; remote-owner projects with weekly evidence; maestros with confirmed portfolios.

### Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Weak construction cycle | Focus on self-build and renovation |
| Lead-marketplace habits | No per-lead fees; free claims |
| Security | Coarse public locations |
| New data protection authority | Counsel review; conservative defaults |

### Approvals before going live

Operator and screening; counsel on terms, privacy notice (2025 law), ComprasMX and OCDS reuse, outreach mode, agent disclosure; provider activations; the hub owner's switch (and the repository owner's for a Contribuda-operated hub).

### Feature gaps for Mexico

CTRY-MX (amended), PLAN-004, PLAN-005, REP-003, PROC-003 (subnational OCDS), REG-002 (RFC validation, contractor registers), PAY-003 (SPEI through licensed providers).
