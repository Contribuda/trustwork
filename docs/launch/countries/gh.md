# Ghana: deep dive and launch plan

Rank 4 of 10 (score 80, estimate, medium uncertainty; [scorecard](../../research/top-10-launch-countries-2026-11.md#scorecard)). Status: **Proposed** playbook. Nothing here is implemented or legal advice; every legal point is a lead for counsel. Sources were accessed on 2026-10-31 unless stated; *(secondary)* marks press, law-firm notes and aggregators. Read the [generic playbook](../README.md) first.

## Summary

Ghana is **stable, English-speaking, growing (6.0 percent in 2025), mobile-money native (81 percent account ownership) and short of about 1.8 million homes**. Dozens of small artisan apps prove the demand, including one backed by German development cooperation and the engineers' institution, but none works at national scale or combines sourced company pages, owned reputation and plans: they are partners more than rivals. Ghana also has something most countries lack, a national digital address for every 5 by 5 metre square (GhanaPostGPS). The weak point is data: contractor classification is issued by ministries and was not found as a public searchable register, so a Ghana hub starts with the company registry, procurement data and claims rather than licence pages.

|---|---|
| Suggested first territory | Greater Accra (Accra and Tema), then Kumasi and Takoradi as city hubs |
| Languages | English (`en`, live); Akan (Twi) as a later community language (no package yet) |

## 4. Culture and trust

- **Finding builders:** family, church, the "mason I know", building-material shops and WhatsApp. Relatives abroad send money for each stage and want proof of progress.
- **Digital habits:** 26.3 million internet users (74.6 percent), 41.8 million connections; Facebook and YouTube each reach about 8.5 million ([DataReportal Digital 2026 Ghana](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-ghana)). WhatsApp is the everyday channel (not reported by DataReportal).
- **Languages and literacy:** English official; Akan (Twi), Ewe, Ga, Dagbani and others widely spoken; adult literacy 76.5 percent (World Bank WDI, 2021). Voice notes help.
- **Addresses:** GhanaPostGPS gives every 5 by 5 metre square a code like `GA-543-0125`; use it for site locations privately and coarse areas publicly (ADDR-001) ([GhanaPostGPS](https://www.ghanapostgps.com); [Graphic Online](https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/how-the-national-digital-property-address-system-works.html) *(secondary)*).
- **Gender and inclusion:** artisan programmes target youth employment; keep gender data optional and aggregate.
- **Calendar:** public holidays to be sourced in CTRY-GH; two rainy seasons in the south (roughly April to July and September to November).

## 5. Law and compliance (for counsel)

| Topic | What we found | Source | Counsel question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company registration | Office of the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992); basic name search online, fuller data by paid search (secondary sources disagree on depth) | [ORC](https://orc.gov.gh/); [Infoproff](https://www.infoproff.com/en/open-data/ghana/216/business-register-in-ghana) *(secondary)* | Reuse of ORC search results on sourced pages |
| Contractor classification | Building and civil works need a Ministry of Works and Housing classification certificate (tenders ask for classes such as D3K3); roads are classified by the Ministry of Roads and Highways; no public searchable register found | [Ministry of Roads and Highways, contractor classification](https://mrh.gov.gh/publications/contractor-classification/); [PPA tender example](https://tenders.ppa.gov.gh/tenders/28448) | Whether a construction authority law is in force and whether lists are public |
| Public procurement | Public Procurement Authority; OCDS publication listed as monthly | [OCDS registry](https://data.open-contracting.org/en/search/) | Reuse licence |
| Tax | VAT Act, 2025 (Act 1151) from 1 January 2026: effective rate about 20 percent, COVID-19 levy abolished, flat rate scheme abolished, registration threshold GHS 750,000 | [Graphic Online](https://www.graphic.com.gh/business/business-news/gra-announces-overhaul-vat-rate-cut-flat-rate-scheme-scrapped-covid-levy-abolished-from-jan-1.html) *(secondary)*; [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/ghanas-finance-minister-presents-2026-budget-statement-and-economic-policy) *(secondary)* | Operator's VAT position if it charges fees |
| Data protection | Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and the Data Protection Commission; registration of controllers (to be sourced) | To source | Registration; direct marketing to business contacts |
| Identity | Ghana Card used widely for identity and tax (to be sourced) | To source | Verification only through licensed verifiers (IDV-001 amended) |
| E-signature, consumer protection | To be sourced | — | Signatory tie; paid services |

- WhatsApp channel for consented notifications (CHAN-001); USSD for basic phones (CHAN-002).

### Hub and territory setup

| Wizard step | Value |
|---|---|
| Starting point | Generic ISO profile for `GH`; local additions: company registration number and Ghana Card based TIN as candidates |
| Territory | Country `GH`; region `GH-AA` (Greater Accra) for the first hub |
| Hub name | "Trustwork Ghana" |
| Languages | `en` |
| Currency, time zone | GHS, `Africa/Accra` |
| Residency | Counsel to confirm requirements under Act 843 |

### The operator team for Ghana

| Function | AI roles from day one | Local human needed first | When to hire |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial | Writer, Fact-checker, SEO (Auto with review) | Editor-in-chief | Day one |
| Data | Collector on OCDS and GSS releases (Assisted) | Data steward | Founder, then a hire |
| Trust and claims | Claim assistant: ORC record, classification certificate as evidence for a person to check, domain e-mail (Draft) | Trust reviewer who knows ministry certificates | Week 10 |
| Partnerships and events | Newsletter and social | Partnerships lead (artisan programmes, engineers' institution) | Week 1 (founder) |
| Support | Support triage with WhatsApp and SMS fallbacks | Support agent who speaks Twi | Week 10 |
| Compliance | Licence checker, Sanctions review assistant | Compliance officer; Ghanaian counsel; DPC registration | Day one |

### Agent team tasks specific to Ghana

- **Discovery:** companies from PPA awards and ORC where terms allow; no licence facts until a public classification source exists; manufacturer and supplier pages from public sites.
- **Editorial:** housing deficit and policy news, building cost index monthly, VAT changes for builders; guides for building over several years with money from abroad.
- **Claims:** ORC record, ministry classification certificate inspected by a person (an uploaded certificate is evidence for review, never proof by itself), signatory tie.

### Outreach policy settings

`strict`; counsel to advise whether a page notice to a company's generic address is direct marketing under Act 843. No SMS, calls or WhatsApp outreach without recorded prior consent.

### Seed content plan

| Plan stage | Ghana-specific seed guides |
|---|---|
| `stage.define` | Building over several years; managing a build from abroad |
| `stage.site_and_feasibility` | Land documentation and disputes; site checks |
| `stage.permitting` | Assembly building permits (taxonomy file `GH.json`) |
| `stage.contracting` | Choosing artisans; checking a contractor's registration and classification |
| `stage.structure` | Blocks, columns and slabs; curing in the heat |
| `stage.handover` | Records for family and future sale |

### Costs (estimates)

The [generic cost model](../README.md#costs); add manual ORC search allowances for claims and WhatsApp or SMS aggregator quotes.

### KPIs

Generic KPIs plus: artisans with credentials from training programmes; remote-owner projects with weekly evidence; claims decided with a classification certificate checked by a person.

### Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| No public contractor register | Claims rely on ORC plus reviewed certificates; label "unknown" honestly |
| Confusion with escrow apps | Clear statement that the hub never holds money; licensed providers only |
| Stale registry records | Show observed dates; watcher re-checks |

### Approvals before going live

Operator and screening; counsel on terms, privacy notice, Act 843 duties, outreach mode, data reuse (ORC, PPA), agent disclosure; DPC registration; provider activations by a second person; the hub owner's switch (and the repository owner's for a Contribuda-operated hub).

### Feature gaps for Ghana

CTRY-GH, ADDR-001 (GhanaPostGPS), PLAN-005, PLAN-004, REP-003, PAY-003, CHAN-002, ACCT-001, IDV-001 amended (Ghana Card through licensed verifiers), PROC-003 (PPA OCDS).
