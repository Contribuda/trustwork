# Colombia: deep dive and launch plan

Rank 5 of 10 (score 80, estimate, medium uncertainty; [scorecard](../../research/top-10-launch-countries-2026-11.md#scorecard)). Status: **Proposed** playbook. Nothing here is implemented or legal advice; every legal point is a lead for counsel. Sources were accessed on 2026-10-31 unless stated; *(secondary)* marks press, law-firm notes and aggregators. Read the [generic playbook](../README.md) first.

## Summary

Colombia has **the best open procurement data on this list (SECOP II, published daily in OCDS through an open API), a new instant-payment system adopted at record speed (Bre-B, more than 100 million registered keys by April 2026), Spanish as a live interface language, and strong industry institutions** (a construction chamber, an engineering council with public registration, and a national training service that certifies construction workers). Informality in construction labour is high (around 60 percent in the competitive landscape's source). The weak point is the cycle: building construction contracted sharply in 2025, so the hub should lead with renovation, self-build guidance, public works opportunities and reputation for small firms, and expect housing to recover. Competitors are adjacent (a large hardware-store software company, older lead marketplaces), not direct.

## 4. Culture and trust

- **Finding builders:** referrals, the "maestro" of the neighbourhood, hardware stores, Facebook and WhatsApp groups. Formal hiring requires checking that contractors pay their own health and pension contributions ([Colombia Move](https://colombiamove.com/blog/es/hiring-colombia-small-business-find-talent/) *(secondary)*), a point many homeowners do not know.
- **Digital habits:** 41.7 million internet users (77.8 percent), 83.0 million connections, Facebook ad reach 36.4 million, TikTok 93 percent of adults, median mobile download 36.76 Mbps ([DataReportal Digital 2026 Colombia](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-colombia)).
- **Language and literacy:** Spanish; adult literacy 95.3 percent (World Bank WDI, 2024).
- **Contact etiquette:** the "Dejen de fregar" law (Law 2300 of 2023) limits the channels, hours and frequency of contact by financial entities and others for collections and marketing; people expect not to be chased ([El Colombiano](https://www.elcolombiano.com/negocios/en-vigor-la-ley-dejen-de-fregar-en-colombia-DE22628957) *(secondary)*).
- **Security:** extortion and armed groups affect construction in some regions; never expose active site locations, values or schedules (ADDR-001).
- **Calendar:** many Monday holidays ("puentes"), Holy Week, year-end holidays; two rainy seasons in the Andean region (roughly April to May and October to November).

## 8. Infrastructure realities

- Urban connectivity is good; rural and conflict-affected areas less so: offline drafts and low-bandwidth pages.
- WhatsApp for consented notifications; e-mail common among firms.
- Addresses follow a grid ("Calle 45 # 12-30") in cities; informal neighbourhoods less so.

## 9. Partners to approach (kinds only)

- The construction chamber and its regional chapters; engineering and architecture associations.
- The national training service (certified workers, course content).
- Chambers of commerce (registry knowledge, events).
- Universities; housing NGOs; hardware-store networks (knowledge partners, never paid ranking).

### Agent team tasks specific to Colombia

- **Discovery:** daily SECOP OCDS import of works processes into the needs board; awards build sourced track records for companies, linked by NIT only; natural-person contractors never get public pages.
- **Editorial:** monthly construction indicators (DANE, the chamber), Bre-B and payments for builders, safety and formal hiring guides.
- **Claims:** RUES and RUP records, signatory tie by electronic signature or call-back.

### Outreach policy settings

`strict`. Counsel to advise on Law 1581 and Law 2300 before any page notice. No SMS, WhatsApp or calls without recorded prior consent.

### Costs (estimates)

The [generic cost model](../README.md#costs); SECOP data is structured, so pages need little model work (low end); add RUES certificate costs for claims if reviewers need them.

### KPIs

Generic KPIs plus: small contractors that viewed a public opportunity and claimed their page (counts); companies with a SECOP-sourced track record.

### Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Building downturn | Lead with renovation, public works and reputation; housing recovery as upside |
| Security and extortion | No active site details on public pages |
| Contact rules | `strict` outreach |

### Approvals before going live

Operator and screening; counsel on terms, privacy notice and database registration (Law 1581), Law 2300, SECOP and RUES reuse, outreach mode, agent disclosure; provider activations; the hub owner's switch (and the repository owner's for a Contribuda-operated hub).

### Feature gaps for Colombia

CTRY-CO, PROC-003 (SECOP II OCDS), REG-002 (RUES and RUP, council checks), PAY-003 (Bre-B through licensed providers), REP-003, PLAN-004, PLAN-005 (families abroad).
