# Kenya: deep dive and launch plan

Rank 3 of 10 (score 81, estimate, low uncertainty; [scorecard](../../research/top-10-launch-countries-2026-11.md#scorecard)). Status: **Proposed** playbook. Nothing here is implemented or legal advice; every legal point is a lead for counsel. Sources were accessed on 2026-10-31 unless stated; *(secondary)* marks press, law-firm notes and aggregators. Read the [generic playbook](../README.md) first.

## Summary

Kenya has **a construction rebound (6.8 percent growth in 2025, cement consumption up about 20 percent), the deepest mobile-money culture in the world (90 percent account ownership), a public contractor register (the National Construction Authority, NCA) and daily OCDS procurement data**. English is a live interface language and Swahili (proposed LANG-SW) opens Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda later. Competitors are small and fragmented: a well-funded materials marketplace (Jumba) and several fundi apps, none with owned reputation or plans. Two things need care: the Data Protection Commissioner enforces **express consent for commercial use of personal data** (section 37) with fines, so outreach starts `strict`; and Kenya is under FATF increased monitoring, which matters for payment partners.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Suggested first territory | Nairobi City County, then Mombasa, Kisumu and Nakuru as city hubs |
| Languages | English (`en`, live) default; Swahili (`sw`, proposed LANG-SW) second |
| Currency, time zone | KES; `Africa/Nairobi` |
| Country pack | Proposed CTRY-KE |
| Suggested first outreach mode after counsel | `strict` (organisation-only notices are a counsel question) |
| Fastest growth path | NCA-registered contractors claiming pages, training-body graduates (fundis) building credentials, professional bodies, county-level content |

## 2. Pain points

| Who | Pain | What Trustwork offers |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowners | Unregistered contractors, poor workmanship, building collapses in informal settlements; materials bought without quantities | NCA registration shown as a sourced fact with category and status; plans with materials and budget ranges; consultations with registered engineers; progressive plans (PLAN-004) |
| Fundis | Work through word of mouth and site-gate hiring; no portfolio; certificates invisible | A personal profile for life; client-confirmed portfolio; trade test and training certificates as credentials (REP-003) |
| Contractors | NCA renewal and continuous professional development obligations; private clients cannot see their category | Claimable sourced page with NCA category and expiry watch (REG-001, REG-002) |
| Suppliers | Price competition; credit risk | Supplier pages with guides; RFQs with frozen quotes; payments only through licensed providers |
| Public buyers | A new e-procurement system contested in court | Opportunities from OCDS data, never bid submission |

## 4. Culture and trust

- **Finding builders:** referrals from family and church, the "fundi" network around hardware stores and building sites, WhatsApp groups and Facebook. Chamas (savings groups) often finance stages of building.
- **Digital habits:** 23.4 million internet users (40.5 percent), 77.5 million mobile connections (134 percent), TikTok reaching 56 percent of adults, Facebook 17.0 million ([DataReportal Digital 2026 Kenya](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kenya)); the World Bank's ITU figure is 35 percent. Many fundis use basic or entry-level phones: SMS and USSD matter (CHAN-002).
- **Languages:** English and Swahili are official; Sheng in Nairobi; many community languages. Swahili content reaches far more fundis than English.
- **Gender and inclusion:** public procurement reserves opportunities for youth, women and persons with disabilities (AGPO; to be sourced); a hub can show AGPO-eligible opportunities if the source allows, without inferring anyone's status.
- **Calendar:** national holidays (Madaraka Day, Mashujaa Day, Jamhuri Day, Eid, Easter, Christmas; to be sourced in CTRY-KE); long rains around March to May and short rains around October to December slow building.

| Rail | Notes |
|---|---|
| M-Pesa | Mobile money used by most adults; developer API (Daraja) with asynchronous push-to-phone authorisation: PAY-001 is amended so the port handles that flow; PAY-003 adds the adapter with a fake |
| Airtel Money | Second mobile-money network |
| PesaLink and cards | Interbank instant transfers; card acquirers and pan-African gateways (candidates in PAY-003) |

Account ownership 90.1 percent (World Bank Findex 2024). Trustwork never holds money; a fundi paid weekly in M-Pesa can attach payment confirmations as private evidence of a task, never as a public figure.

## 8. Infrastructure realities

- Smartphone and data costs limit use outside cities; SMS and USSD reach basic phones (CHAN-002); low-bandwidth pages (MOB-001).
- Median fixed download speed 15.44 Mbps ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kenya)); mobile data dominates.
- Plot numbers, estate names and landmarks instead of street addresses (ADDR-001).
- Sender IDs and short codes for SMS are registered with operators and the communications regulator (to be sourced for CHAN-002).

## 9. Partners to approach (kinds only)

- Contractor and builder associations; the NCA's training arm (continuous professional development partners).
- Engineers', architects' and quantity surveyors' bodies.
- Technical and vocational training institutions and trade-test bodies (worker credentials).
- Universities with built-environment faculties; international organisations based in Nairobi working on housing.
- Savings groups and housing cooperatives (progressive building education).

### The operator team for Kenya

| Function | AI roles from day one | Local human needed first | When to hire |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial | Writer, Fact-checker, Translator to Swahili (Auto with review) | Editor-in-chief; Swahili reviewer | Day one; Swahili reviewer at week 7 |

| Trust and claims | Claim assistant: NCA category and status, business registry, domain e-mail, screening (Draft) | Trust reviewer | Week 10 |

| Compliance | Licence checker; Sanctions review assistant | Compliance officer; Kenyan counsel; ODPC registration | Day one |
| Events and learning | Event listings (Draft) | Partner trainers for continuous professional development sessions | Week 11 |

### Agent team tasks specific to Kenya

- **Discovery:** OCDS awards to build track records for companies; NCA status for companies that have a sourced page or claim; never pages for individual fundis.
- **Editorial:** NCA notices, county approvals news, housing programme updates, material prices; Swahili editions.
- **Claims:** NCA category and status, registry record, domain e-mail or signatory tie; potential sanctions matches to human review.
- **Fundis:** onboarding by SMS or USSD (CHAN-002, ACCT-001), credential upload from training bodies, client confirmations after tasks.

### Outreach policy settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Mode | `strict` (also the discovery design's suggestion for Kenya). Organisation-only notices only if counsel concludes section 37 does not apply to generic company addresses |
| Channels | No SMS, calls or WhatsApp outreach; e-mail only if ever allowed |
| Registrations | ODPC controller registration where thresholds apply |

### Seed content plan

| Plan stage | Kenya-specific seed guides |
|---|---|
| `stage.define` | Building in stages with savings groups; budgeting each stage |
| `stage.site_and_feasibility` | Title deeds and land searches; soil and drainage in the rainy seasons |
| `stage.permitting` | County approvals and NCA project registration (taxonomy file `KE.json`) |
| `stage.contracting` | Checking an NCA category; agreeing fundi rates and milestones |
| `stage.foundation`, `stage.structure` | Concrete, masonry and reinforcement basics; avoiding collapses |
| `stage.handover` | Records and warranties |

### Costs (estimates)

The [generic cost model](../README.md#costs); add SMS or USSD aggregator quotes for fundi onboarding and verification codes, and a Swahili reviewer.

### KPIs

Generic KPIs plus: companies with a current NCA category on their page; fundis with at least one confirmed task; Swahili share of published items.

### Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Section 37 enforcement | `strict` outreach; consent-based channels only |
| FATF increased monitoring | Licensed payment partners with their own due diligence; sanctions gates |
| E-procurement legal uncertainty | Label sources and dates; never imply a system is mandatory |
| Building collapses and liability | Guides cite, never certify; consultations with registered professionals |

### Approvals before going live

Operator and screening; counsel on terms, privacy notice, section 37 and outreach mode, NCA and PPRA data reuse, residency, agent disclosure; ODPC registration; provider activations by a second person; the hub owner's switch (and the repository owner's for a Contribuda-operated hub).

### Feature gaps for Kenya

CTRY-KE, LANG-SW, CHAN-002 (USSD), ACCT-001, PAY-003 (M-Pesa and Airtel Money through licensed integrations), PAY-001 amended (asynchronous push-to-phone), REP-003, REG-002 (NCA connector), PROC-003 (PPRA OCDS), ADDR-001, PLAN-004.
