# How it works for experts and mentors

You are a structural engineer, a building inspector, an energy rater, a seasoned superintendent or a veteran roofer, and people keep asking you for advice. Trustwork lets you share that experience on your terms: consultations, expert answers, mentoring and courses, with reputation and income that follow from what you actually delivered.

Status labels are explained in the [guides index](README.md#status-labels).

## Step by step

| # | Step | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Set up your profile and credentials** | Your profile, your licences (for example a professional-engineer licence, shown only after the hub's check passed) and your areas of expertise. Without a passed check you are shown as an experienced practitioner, never as a licensed professional. | Profile editor is **Demo**; credentials and checks are **Proposed** (REP-001, CLM-001) |
| 2 | **Publish consultation offerings** | Advice sessions, drawing reviews, quote reviews, site visits, mentoring series. Free, fixed price or hourly, each a versioned offer; a booking pins the exact version. | **Proposed** (CONS-001; paid offerings CONS-002) |
| 3 | **Set your availability** | Calendar windows and capacity. Your agent filters incoming requests by scope, region, languages and calendar and proposes slots. | **Proposed** (CONS-001, AGT-022) |
| 4 | **Hold the consultation** | Video, chat, phone or in person. Recording only with both sides' consent. A homeowner's address is revealed only for a booked site visit. | **Proposed** (CONS-001, COMMS-001) |
| 5 | **Deliver your note** | Your agent drafts the deliverable from your session notes; you finish and sign it. The client accepts it; delivery and acceptance are separate records. | **Proposed** (CONS-001) |
| 6 | **Get paid** | Through the hub's licensed payment provider; the hub's commission for consultations is a published, versioned setting shown to both sides before booking. Trustwork never holds the money. | **Proposed** (CONS-002, PAY-001, BILL-003) |
| 7 | **Answer questions in public** | Expert answers are publications with your name, sources and corrections. | **Proposed** (KNOW-001; publications are **Contract**) |
| 8 | **Mentor** | A mentoring relationship is double opt-in; either side may end it; sessions are consultations of kind mentoring. | **Proposed** (CONS-001) |
| 9 | **Teach a course** | Short practical courses with assessments and certificates anyone can check by number. | Course pages and the certificate check are **Demo**; authoring is **Proposed** (LRN-001) |
| 10 | **Earn reputation from evidence** | Consultations delivered and accepted, answers that others confirmed useful through their own projects, courses taught. Never by volume, never for money. | **Proposed** (REP-001) |

## Fair rules for experts

- Reviews exist only for completed bookings and cite the real deliverable; unconfirmed reviews are labelled and excluded from aggregates.
- Recommendations of experts for a plan step are ranked by fit only; a hub's commission never changes who is suggested.
- Advice that legally requires a licence is offered only by experts whose licence check passed.
