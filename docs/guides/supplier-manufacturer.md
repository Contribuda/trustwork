# How it works for suppliers and manufacturers

You make or distribute materials and products: lumber, connectors, roofing membranes, windows, insulation. On Trustwork you are not only a seller; you are a source of practical knowledge. People planning a project see your guides, training and products next to the step where they need them, because they fit, never because you paid.

Status labels are explained in the [guides index](README.md#status-labels).

## Step by step

| # | Step | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Find your sourced page** | The hub's editorial office may already have built a page about your company from your public website, within the hub's legal basis and your site's licence and robots rules. It is labelled "from sources" and lists your catalogues, guides, training and events as links. | **Proposed** (MAT-001, EDIT-001, SRC-001) |
| 2 | **Claim and verify it** | Claim the page, pass the hub's verification recipe, and correct any sourced facts; every correction keeps its provenance. Until you claim it, nobody may use the page for bulk outreach to you. | **Proposed** (CLM-001, MAT-001) |
| 3 | **Keep your catalogue** | Versioned catalogue offerings with technical data and documents. A quote you sent never changes when your catalogue does. | **Designed** (COM-001) |
| 4 | **Publish guides and training** | Installation guides, technical articles, courses and events, labelled as company-authored. Your installer training can lead to certificates anyone can check. | Publications are **Contract**; courses are **Proposed** (LRN-001); events **Proposed** (EVT-001) |
| 5 | **Appear on the right plan steps** | When someone's plan reaches "frame the house", the plan-step companion may suggest your connectors and your installation guide, with an explanation of why they fit. Ranking is by fit only; a hub may allow labelled sponsored content, off by default, shown separately and never used for ranking or recommendations. | **Proposed** (REC-001, AGT-021; sponsored content rule in ADR 012 decision 3) |
| 6 | **Answer step questions** | People can ask you a question about a step, anonymously by default. You see the question, never their project, address or budget. Your answers cite your published documents and are labelled as coming from a party with a commercial interest. | **Proposed** (AGT-021, MAT-001) |
| 7 | **Quote and deliver** | Answer RFQs with immutable quote versions; record deliveries; ask for confirmations. | Quote versions are **Contract**; flows **Designed** (COM-001, COM-002) |
| 8 | **Build your reputation** | On-time deliveries confirmed by customers, quote response times, product certificates and test reports, courses taught and answers others found useful. | **Proposed** (REP-001) |

## Fair rules for suppliers

- Recommendations are never paid for; payment never changes ranking, matching, recommendations or reputation.
- Sourced stays sourced until you claim the page; claimed and verified look different.
- Being recommended never exposes the asker's private plan to you.
