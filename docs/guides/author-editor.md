# How it works for authors and editors

You write about construction: a practitioner sharing what went wrong on a framing inspection, a trade journalist, a company's technical writer, or the editor-in-chief of a hub. Trustwork treats content with the discipline of a good newsroom: named sources, honest dates, visible corrections, and authorship that is never confused with editing rights.

Status labels are explained in the [guides index](README.md#status-labels).

## For authors

| # | Step | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **See how readers read** | News, articles, topics, rubrics, the weekly magazine with its reader and RSS feeds. | **Demo** |
| 2 | **Write a publication** | Personal, company or editorial authorship; a format (news, article, post) and an article format (analysis, practice, overview, experience, interview, project story); topics; links to companies, people, projects and events. AI-assisted writing is labelled. | The workspace publications list is **Demo**; the publication model is **Contract**; authoring is **Designed** (CNT-001) |
| 3 | **Send it for review** | Where the hub's policy requires review, an editor approves or returns it. | **Designed** (CNT-001) |
| 4 | **Publish** | A published version never changes. Your wall and your company's wall reference the same version, never a copy. | **Contract**; backend **Designed** (CNT-001, CNT-003) |
| 5 | **Correct openly** | A correction is a new version with a note readers can see; every wall and magazine issue that points to it shows the correction. | **Contract** |
| 6 | **Answer and share knowledge** | Expert answers, checklists and project diaries are publications too, with your name and corrections. | **Proposed** (KNOW-001) |
| 7 | **Build your reputation** | Publications with sources, corrections made promptly, answers others confirmed useful. | **Proposed** (REP-001) |

## For editors

| # | Step | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Register sources** | Feeds, publisher APIs, official announcements, procurement notices, each with licence flags (display, summary, full text, index, model egress, syndication). | **Proposed** (SRC-001) |
| 2 | **Review the queue** | Imported items and AI drafts arrive as drafts. Nothing is published without a person's decision. | **Proposed** (EDIT-001, PIPE-001) |
| 3 | **Schedule, publish, correct, retract** | The publications board by state, author type, format and topic. | **Designed** (CNT-001); console screens **Proposed** (HUBC-003) |
| 4 | **Assemble the magazine** | A numbered, dated issue freezes an ordered list of publication versions; the web reader and the PDF are separate outputs. | Reader is **Demo**; PDF layout is **Designed** (CNT-003) |
| 5 | **Translate** | Linked editions per language with translator credit; machine-assisted translations are labelled until a person reviews them. | **Proposed** (CNT-001, PROV-001) |
