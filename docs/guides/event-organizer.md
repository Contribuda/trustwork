# How it works for event organizers

You run a building-science meetup in Denver, a trade association's annual conference, a manufacturer's installer day or a site visit for students. Trustwork lists your event where the right people look for it, handles registration and tickets, and helps attendees meet each other.

Status labels are explained in the [guides index](README.md#status-labels).

## Step by step

| # | Step | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Browse how attendees see events** | Event list with filters, event pages, registration, ticket and calendar file. | **Demo** (`/events`; the payment step is an imitation that charges nothing) |
| 2 | **Create your event** | As a person, a company or the hub's editorial team: date, place or link, programme, speakers (shown only with their consent), capacity. | **Proposed** (EVT-001, EVT-002) |
| 3 | **Set tickets** | Free or paid tickets as versioned prices. Paid tickets go through the hub's licensed payment provider; the hub's ticket fee is a published, versioned setting. Trustwork never holds the money. | **Proposed** (EVT-001, PAY-001, BILL-003) |
| 4 | **Open registration** | Attendees register in a minute and receive a ticket. | Registration and ticket screens are **Demo**; the backend is **Proposed** (EVT-001) |
| 5 | **Partner meetings** | Attendees ask to meet each other; agents propose slots inside the event schedule; every meeting is double opt-in. | **Proposed** (EVT-001) |
| 6 | **Check people in** | Check-in on a phone at the door, working in bright sunlight and on a weak connection. | **Proposed** (EVT-001) |
| 7 | **Publish materials and payouts** | Slides, recordings (with consent) and notes after the event; payouts to you through the payment provider. | **Proposed** (EVT-001, BILL-002) |
| 8 | **Reach other hubs** | Public events can appear in the calendars of peered hubs, with your home hub named. | **Proposed** (FED-002) |
| 9 | **Build your reputation** | Events held as announced, materials published on time. | **Proposed** (REP-001) |

## Fair rules for organizers

- Listings are ordered by relevance and date, never by payment; sponsorship, if the hub allows it, is labelled.
- Attendee lists are private to the organizer and never sold; contact between attendees needs consent on both sides.
