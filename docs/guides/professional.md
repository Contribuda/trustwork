# How it works for professionals and contractors

You are an electrician, a framer, a site superintendent, an estimator, an architect or a small contractor. Trustwork gives you a profile that belongs to you for life, tools to **build and confirm your own reputation** from real work, and an agent that helps you find and answer the right requests without spending your evenings on paperwork.

Status labels are explained in the [guides index](README.md#status-labels).

## Step by step

| # | Step | What happens | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Create your account** | One account for life, independent of any employer. | **Live** in the API (verification mail through the worker; no mail server is configured in the demo); screens are **Demo** |
| 2 | **Fill in your public profile** | Name, trade, region, experience, languages. You choose whether the profile is public at all and preview exactly what others see. | The public profile editor on "My page" is **Demo**; profiles with consent records are **Proposed** (PPL-001) |
| 3 | **Join your employer** | Your company invites you; you accept. One active employer at a time; when you leave, your own profile and publications stay with you and the company's data stays with the company. | Memberships (invite, accept, change, end) are **Live** in the API |
| 4 | **Add credentials** | State licences, insurance certificates, manufacturer certifications, course certificates. The hub checks what it can and shows "checked on date by hub X"; the documents themselves are never shown publicly. Your agent reminds you 60, 30 and 7 days before something expires. | Platform certificates on "My page" and the public certificate check are **Demo**; credentials and checks are **Proposed** (REP-001, CLM-001, HUB-003) |
| 5 | **Build your portfolio** | Finished jobs with photos, inspection results and before/after. An entry says "client confirmed" only when the client confirmed it; otherwise it says "self-reported". | **Proposed** (REP-001, WEB-008) |
| 6 | **Find and answer requests** | Your agent watches tasks and requests that fit your trade, region, licence and calendar, drafts an offer from your price list, and sends it only within the mandate you gave it. Your floor price stays private. | Procurement and sales lists are **Demo**; tasks are **Proposed** (TASK-001); offers as immutable quote versions are **Contract** |
| 7 | **Do the work and record it** | Record milestones with photos and documents; ask the client to confirm. A confirmation from the client is a separate record from your claim. | Delivery claims and acceptance are **Contract**; execution is **Designed** (EXE-001); confirmation requests are **Proposed** (REP-001) |
| 8 | **Watch your reputation grow** | Confirmed jobs, on-time delivery against the plan as it was agreed (client-caused changes do not count against you), reviews tied to real jobs, your answers and courses. Every number shows its method, sample size and exclusions. | Reputation snapshots are **Designed** (EXE-001); the reputation builder toolkit is **Proposed** (REP-001, AGT-005) |
| 9 | **Carry it with you** | A reputation passport of verifiable credentials (W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0, compatible with Open Badges 3.0) that you can show on any hub or outside Trustwork. You choose what goes in. | **Proposed** (REP-002) |
| 10 | **Disagree fairly** | Dispute an observation or appeal a review with evidence; a different person decides. You can respond to fair criticism; you cannot remove it. | Disputes and appeals are **Designed** (EXE-001) |

## What nobody can do to your reputation

- Buy it, boost it or sell it: no plan, payment, sponsorship or staff action changes a score, badge or passport credential.
- Turn it into surveillance: reputation is evidence you bring and confirm, never a productivity dashboard for your employer.
- Your agent's coaching suggests only legitimate actions ("two finished jobs are waiting for client confirmation"); it never suggests review swaps or incentives.
