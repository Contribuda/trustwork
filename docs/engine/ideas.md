# Ideas ranked by impact

Status: **Proposed**. Ideas added to the specification on 2026-10-31 from the [worldwide competitive landscape](../research/competitive-landscape-2026-11.md), the earlier [market scan](../research/market-and-ideas-2026-10.md) and the specification work itself. Each idea is already written into the module page named in the table, with its requirement identifiers and work package, so that it can be reviewed, accepted, changed or rejected like any other design. Nothing here is implemented.

Ranking: **I** = impact on people and on a hub's honest growth (1 to 5); **E** = effort for this codebase as it stands (1 to 5); sorted by impact, then by lower effort. "Source" says where the idea was learned; "own" marks ideas from the specification work without a single precedent.

## Own ideas below the cut (not yet in the specification)

These are recorded so they are not lost. Each needs an owner decision or a design note before it enters a module page and gets a package.

| Idea | Why it could matter | Risk to weigh |
|---|---|---|
| **Disaster-response mode for a hub**: after a storm or flood, a temporary hub setting raises licence-check visibility, applies stricter deposit templates, shows consumer warnings about door-to-door offers, and prioritises verified local contractors in explanations (never in paid positions) | Contractor fraud after disasters harms the people least able to absorb it; a gathering point can be useful exactly then | Must not become a ranking control; time-limited, second-person activation, published |
| **Offer completeness checklist**: each offer shows what is missing compared with the request (taxes not stated, exclusions not listed, schedule absent) without scoring the seller | Clients compare apples with oranges today; sellers learn what to state | Must stay explanatory, never a hidden score |
| **Change impact preview**: before a change order is accepted, both sides see the effect on plan dates, budget range and who bears the delay | Prevents disputes about timing reputation later | Needs reliable plan dependencies first |
| **Provenance for field photographs** (content credentials attached at capture in the mobile web) | Evidence that cannot be quietly swapped strengthens confirmations and disputes | Device support varies; never a reason to distrust photos without credentials |
| **Apprenticeship pathways**: courses, mentoring and supervised tasks linked into a path a newcomer can show | Trades need newcomers; reputation for learning progress the learner controls | Must not become employer surveillance or a hiring score |
| **Public materials price indices** from official statistics as sourced context on plan budget ranges | Honest context for budgets without private quotes | Licence and freshness of statistics per country |

## How ideas move forward

1. Review the module page and the package in a pull request.
2. Where an idea touches money, ranking, reputation, authority, quote immutability or public projections, it needs integrator review and, where the [ADR 012 question list](../adr/012-integrator-decisions-2026-10-31.md#questions-that-remain-for-the-owner-or-counsel) applies, the owner or counsel.
3. Accepted ideas keep their requirement identifiers; rejected ones are marked **Withdrawn** with a reason, never deleted.
