# Experts and mentors

Experienced practitioners who share what they know: structural and building-services engineers, architects, estimators, building-science specialists, inspectors, veteran tradespeople. Guide: [Experts and mentors](../../guides/expert.md). Agent: [editor, author or expert](../agent-catalog.md#12-editor-author-or-expert), [consultation](../agent-catalog.md#consultation).

## What they come for

To be paid and recognised for advice: consultations from people's plans, expert answers that others reuse, mentoring newcomers, courses, talks; reputation built from accepted deliverables and confirmed usefulness, not from volume or stars.

## What they build

Consultations delivered and accepted, answers others confirmed useful through their own projects, mentoring delivered, courses taught; licence checks; on-time deliverables.

## What can never happen to them

Stars without a booking; reviews from people who were not clients; a lower position because they charge nothing or the hub earns nothing from them; their calendar exposed beyond proposed slots.

## Country and jurisdiction variations

Which advice requires a licence (stamped drawings, legal advice), recording consent rules, invoicing duties and tax on fees, cross-border advice restrictions.

## Their own metrics

Requests, bookings, deliverable acceptance, on-time delivery, earnings, reuse of their answers.

## Open points

Commission levels for consultations in the reference hub; whether mentoring sessions may be bundled as subscriptions (fair-subscription controls apply).
